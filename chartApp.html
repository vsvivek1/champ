What the commentary means

⚡ Speed (first item)

Avg range of the last 3 candles vs the normal range (prior 20 candles). MOVING FAST ≥2× = candles twice their normal size — momentum, your profit zone (amber blink + high double-beep). dead chart ≤0.35× = nothing moving (low beep once). In between shows as plain speed 1.1×.

⏱ Replay a point in time

Click any candle on any chart — the strip below shows what the commentary said at that moment (⏱ at HH:MM + speed, trend, OI, pattern…), with an amber dashed border. It holds 20s; click the strip to jump back to live.

Trend

uptrend (3/5 green, HH+HL): of the last 5 completed 1-min candles, 3 closed up; chart makes Higher Highs + Higher Lows. downtrend (LH+LL) is the mirror. Otherwise sideways.

% vs open

Premium/spot vs today's 09:15 open. -54% vs open = option lost half its value today.

VWAP

Volume-weighted avg price of the day = where the average rupee traded. Above = buyers of the day in profit (bullish); below = bearish.

MA20

20-minute moving average. "above MA20 (falling)" = price above the line but the line still slopes down — bounce not yet confirmed. Best: above + rising.

Volume

volume spike 2.1× avg = last candle traded twice the 20-candle average — the move has participation. thin volume = ≤half average, don't trust the move.

OI + price (15 min) — options only

long buildup: price ↑ OI ↑ — fresh buyers.
short covering: price ↑ OI ↓ — writers buying back their shorts; often fuels a sharp up-move (green blink + beep).
short buildup: price ↓ OI ↑ — fresh sellers.
long unwinding: price ↓ OI ↓ — buyers giving up.
🔥 OI CRASH: OI drops ≥2% within 5 min — writers bailing out fast. If price is holding or rising it says "covering rally likely" (green blink + beep: possible sharp up-move). With price falling it's longs dumping — bearish.

brk↑ / brk↓ (breakout & breakdown alerts)

Two spot levels, defaulted to yesterday's high and low on Load (edit them freely — your value survives reloads). Drawn as dashed green/red lines on the spot chart. When spot crosses: high triple-beep + BREAKOUT message (up), low triple-beep + BREAKDOWN (down). Re-arms after spot pulls back ~0.05% inside. Alert only — it places no orders; use a chart-click trigger if you want an automatic order at the level.

tf (timeframe) + full view

Each chart has its own tf picker (top-left, under the title) — so you can run a 5s CE next to a 15m spot. Changing one refetches only that chart. The tf all picker in the header sets the same timeframe on all three at once. 1m/5m/15m = today, 1h = 10 days, day = 180 days (Kite history). 5s/15s/30s are built from live ticks — Kite has no sub-minute history, so they only cover from when the strike was first charted (server keeps the buffer even across page reloads). Commentary indicators (MA20, trend, OI windows) are per-candle, so they get faster/slower with the timeframe. The ⛶ icon on each chart expands it to full screen — Esc or ⛶ again to exit. The trading panel (positions, EXIT ALL, target/SL, messages) moves into the full view with you, and the spot chart's full view also shows CE/PE quick buttons — everything is tradable without leaving it.

auto (IV) strike selection

On by default — opening the page loads the IV-picked strikes with no clicks, and strikes away is greyed out while it is on. Untick it (and Load) to pick strikes by hand.
The OTM strike is picked by: strike = spot ± 0.6 × spot × IV × √(D÷365), rounded to the strike step (50 NIFTY / 100 SENSEX). + for CE, − for PE. D = days to expiry counting today as 1 (0.5 on expiry afternoon, after 12:00). IV comes from India VIX (fallback 0.13 if unavailable). Untick to use "strikes away" manually as before.
After Load, the message line says how far the pick landed from the ATM, measured off the strike actually loaded — so if the computed strike is not listed and snaps to a neighbour, the count follows it.

strikes-away badge (top-left of each option chart)

Says how many strike steps that option is from the money right now, e.g. 4 steps OTM = 4 steps out (200 pts on NIFTY, 400 on SENSEX). ITM green, ATM amber, OTM grey. It is recomputed on every spot tick, not frozen at Load: the ATM moves with spot, so a 4-step OTM call becomes 2 steps OTM once spot runs 100 points your way. Hover it for the strike, the live ATM and the distance in points. Shown in both manual and auto (IV) mode.

open-entry badge (below the strikes-away badge)

Shown only while that option has an open position: entry 12.45 ×75 · +8.4% · +₹785 — the price this entry filled at, the quantity, and the live P&L on this entry. Green when in profit, red when not. The money number updates on every tick (entry and qty refresh every 5s), so it is the P&L right now, not at the last refresh. Entry is the last COMPLETE BUY fill of the day, never Kite's blended average_price. Hover for the full sum. Day P&L for the symbol (realised included) stays in the positions table below. A held strike that is not charted has no badge (there is no chart to put it on) — its live P&L is in the header's entry P&L and in the table. The server keeps every held instrument subscribed either way.

entry P&L (header, next to Day P&L)

Unrealised P&L on the entries open right now, summed: (ltp − entry) × qty per position. It moves on every tick and covers every open F&O position — including a strike that is not one of the two charts on screen, which is why it can be live while a chart is not. Hover it for the per-symbol breakdown. Day P&L next to it is Kite's figure and does include realised P&L, so the two differ after a part-exit. The ltp, % and entry P&L columns of the positions table tick live off the same prices.

positions table after a refresh (the dim rows + amber line)

A reload paints the snapshot this tab saw last immediately, dimmed, with the amber line above the table saying which moment it is from — so a refresh never shows an empty table while the live call is in flight. It goes solid the instant the live positions land. If the amber line instead says positions stale — …retrying, /api/position is failing (Kite aborted the call or rate-limited the reload burst): the rows stay put, they are just not live, and it retries every 1.2s until it gets through.

B100 / B50 / S50 / S100 (top of each option chart)

Emergency buttons. B100/B50 = BUY using 100%/50% of available margin at a marketable limit (LTP+2%). S100 = dump the whole position now (resting order modified to LTP−3%, never a second order). S50 = sell half: the resting SELL's quantity is cut to half and made marketable; the guard re-covers the remaining half within 15s. First click arms (SURE?), second click within 3s fires — regardless of existing orders.

Candle patterns (last completed candle)

doji = open≈close, indecision.
hammer = long lower wick — sellers pushed it down, buyers took it back.
shooting star = long upper wick — the mirror, rejection on top.
bullish engulfing = green candle swallows previous red body.
bearish engulfing = red swallows previous green.

Breakout / range

"breaking above 10-min high" = LTP beyond the extreme of the last 10 completed candles. Spot also shows vs yesterday's high/low.

Lines on the chart

teal dashed TGT = your resting target — drag to modify.
red dashed SL = your resting SL trigger — drag to modify.
amber dashed #n = pending trigger (BUY), red = SELL / EXIT ALL trigger. On the index chart these watch the spot; on a CE/PE chart they watch that option's own price.
orange dotted GUARD SL = the guard's breach level (lowest low of the 3 candles before entry, frozen) — the price where it force-exits. Drag it to move the level (applies live).
white solid ENTRY ×qty = the price your open position was filled at — reference only, not draggable.
blue solid = order being placed — drag it, then confirm in the pop-up.

BUY waits for the price

Clicking a level on a CE/PE chart does not buy now. It parks a one-shot BUY and the server fires it only when that option's own price actually reaches the level — click below LTP to wait for a dip, above to wait for a rise. The button says BUY n if ≤/≥ price; first click arms, second click parks it. It then shows as an amber #n line on that option's chart plus a chip in the panel — cancel with ✕. Drag the blue line before confirming to move the level. Clicking exactly at LTP arms a condition that is already true, so it fires on the next tick. For an immediate entry use the B100/B50 quick buttons instead. Triggers are one-shot, dropped overnight, and survive a restart.

Target / SL — ₹ or %

The ₹ / % dropdown beside the target and SL boxes decides how your number is read. ₹ = an absolute price, as before. % = a percentage off the selected position's entry — target goes above entry, SL below, and the result is rounded to the 0.05 tick. The resolved price is previewed next to the buttons before you send, and the confirmation message reports the price that actually went to the exchange. The SL goes out as an SL-M on the trigger, so there is no separate limit box. % needs a known entry price, so it is rejected on a position that has not filled yet.

Target or SL on this entry

The global default is now fixed at SL first — the panel's old on-fill dropdown and target-% slider are gone, because a resting target was what hard-capped every win. Every click menu still has its own on fill picker, so one entry can rest a target instead if you really want one. Leave it on "panel default" (SL first) for the normal path: exchange SL, in-app partial at the first resistance, stop trailed up behind cleared levels. The choice is shown on the CONFIRM button and on the pending-trigger chip (→ SL first), travels with the entry, and the guard applies it to that one fill only — the next fill is back to the panel default. It is ignored by the SELL/EXIT buttons (they close a position, they do not create a fill), and the B100/B50 quick buttons are one-click by design, so they follow the panel default. An entry whose BUY never fills has its choice retired after 15min.

Auto target

Picking Auto in the target dropdown greys out the box and works the target out from the current situation instead: 2× the distance to your SL (your resting SL trigger if you have one, else the guard's orange breach line), falling back to 2× ATR(14) on that chart's timeframe when there is no SL yet, and to the panel's own target % when the chart is too short for an ATR. That distance is then multiplied by the live speed factor from the commentary strip, clamped to 0.7–2.0 — a fast-moving option gets a wider target, a dead one a tighter one. The preview shows the price and how it was derived (e.g. target → 142.65 (2R off SL 108.30 × speed 1.40)), it refreshes every 2s as the chart moves, and nothing is sent until you click TARGET — it is the same absolute tick-rounded price ₹ mode would send. This is arithmetic on your own numbers, not a view on what you should aim for; the multipliers are the AUTO constants at the top of the script.

Panel

"on fill: SL at -x%" = the stop the guard rests the moment a BUY fills (always in %, clamped to the level just below entry). qty 50 in % mode = spend 50% of available margin. EXIT / EXIT ALL = dump at deep marketable LIMIT regardless of target/SL (two clicks). "% gain / to SL" column = live % from entry, and the % loss if the SL/guard level is hit. "P&L history" = every closed trade (logged by the guard), today's total and per-day totals.
Click the NIFTY/SENSEX chart → "EXIT ALL LONGS if spot ≥/≤" sets an emergency exit level: all longs dumped the moment spot reaches it.

After every trade

The second a position goes flat the page covers itself with the review: that trade's P&L, how it was exited, MFE (the best it ever showed you) and MAE (the worst heat it put you through) tracked tick by tick from the entry fill, how long it was held, how far the guard level sat under entry — and a verdict naming what actually failed: ENTRY (never went 2% green), EXIT (was 15%+ up and gave it back), LEVEL (stop under 1% away — inside the noise), RISK (stop 8%+ away, so one breach is a big loss), TOO EARLY (dead in under 2 min), DEAD TRADE (15+ min sideways — theta rent). Below it: today's trades with their reasons, W/L, per-symbol and day totals. Esc or ✕ closes it; "last trade" reopens it.

⚠ What the orange GUARD SL line is, and is not

The green TGT and red SL lines are real orders sitting at the exchange — they fill even if this app, your laptop or your internet dies. The orange GUARD SL (app-side) line is not an order: it is this process watching ticks and selling when the 3-candle low breaks. It only works while chartApp is running and receiving ticks. If the line reads GUARD SL ⚠ NO ORDER RESTING, the position has no exchange-side exit at all. On 28/10/2026 that is exactly what happened: with firstOrder: target the +50% SELL was above the strike's upper circuit band, the exchange rejected it on every retry, and the guard's only exit path was "modify the resting SELL" — which did not exist. The trade ran to -33% (-₹7,947). Now: the target is clamped into the circuit band, a rejected target falls back to a real SL order, a breach with nothing resting places the exit outright, and an unprotected position is a hard no-trade blocker. For exchange-side protection on every entry, set sl first in the guard bar (guardSettings.json: firstOrder).

Levels: partial out at resistance, then the level becomes the stop

The guard reads this option's own 1-min candles and finds its swing pivots — bars that are the high (or low) of the two bars each side. Pivots within 0.75% of each other are one level, so three taps of 46.0/46.1/46.2 is one level, not three. Those are the grey R lines and the teal S line on the chart.
  • Target = the immediate resistance above entry (at least levelGapPct away, at most levelMaxPct, and always inside the upper circuit band). partialPct of the lot comes off there — the teal PARTIAL line.
  • Stop = a tick under the immediate support, fenced in by the percentages: never tighter than slPct (that would be inside the noise), never wider than slMaxPct (that would not be a stop). slMode: "pct" restores the old flat percentage.
  • Trail — once price clears a resistance by 0.5%, that level becomes the stop: the orange guard line and the resting SL's trigger both move up to it. It only ever moves up, and the Risk column shows trailed ×N.
Why the partial is app-side by default (partialMode: "app"): one SL at the exchange covers the whole lot, and this app sells the partial when the level is reached. The alternative ("exchange") rests a real LIMIT at the level, so the partial fills even if this app dies — but then the stop only covers the remainder and the other half has no exchange-side exit, which is the exact hole that cost ₹7,947 on 28/10. A missed profit-take costs nothing by comparison.

Risk in rupees, on every entry

Before you send a BUY, the menu says what it risks: risk ₹X = (entry − the stop the guard will rest) × qty, with the premium under it. Drag the line or change on fill and the number re-computes. The CONFIRM label and the B50/B100 "SURE?" step carry the same figure, so nothing is sent without the money being on screen. When the first order fills, a 💸 message reports the risk read off the order that actually rested — the real number, not the estimate. The positions table has a Risk column doing the same from the live resting SL. With firstOrder: target there is no exchange-side stop, so every one of these reads the whole premium in red: that is not pessimism, it is what 28/10 cost.

Trade analysis (date wise)

The trade analysis button opens one card per trading day, newest first: an abstract of the day, then the lessons ranked by what they actually cost — the day-loss limit being crossed and what the trades after it added, overtrading past the cap, longest loss streak, tilt re-entries (in and out again within 2 min of a loss), churned strikes, worst hour of the day, and any single trade that was more than half the damage. Under each abstract: every trade of that day with its exit reason and diagnosis. Click a date to fold it open or shut. Days logged before the per-trade review existed show day-level patterns only (no MFE/MAE or hold time).

🔒 10-minute lockout

If the exit was a stop — the SL order triggering, or the guard breaking the 3-candle low — new entries are locked for 10 minutes. B50/B100 grey out, buy triggers refuse to arm, and even an already-parked BUY trigger skips firing; the header counts the lock down. Exits are never blocked — you can always close what you are holding. It survives a restart (tradeLock.json), and the only way out early is POST /api/lock {"action":"clear"}, which is logged, because overriding it is exactly the mistake it exists to stop.
